# NNNN. Short title, in imperative mood (e.g. "Use Postgres for primary storage")

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-short-title.md)

## Context

What problem forced this decision? What constraints, forces, or prior
decisions bear on it? State this neutrally — a reader who ends up
disagreeing with the Decision should still recognize the Context as
accurate.

## Decision

What was decided, stated as an active choice ("We will ..."), not a
passive description.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result — for this codebase and for
whoever works in it next. Include the real trade-offs, not just the
upside.
